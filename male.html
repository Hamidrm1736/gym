How this plan works

  • Train 3 days a week (for example Mon / Wed / Fri) — any days you like, just not back-to-back.
  • Two versions, one plan: in the Workout tab tap 🏋️ Gym or 🏠 Home. The home version uses your bench, barbell, plates and dumbbells and chases the same result — use whichever suits the day.
  • Style: moderate weights, 10–15 reps, stop 1–2 reps before failure. This builds a lean, defined shape — not bulk.
  • Muscle grows slowly — you will look "toned" long before you could ever look "too big".
  • Fat loss happens in the Meal Plan tab. The gym shapes the muscle; food removes the layer over it.
  • First session: use the suggested weight shown under each exercise. Beating the top reps easily → go heavier next set. Can't reach the bottom → go lighter.
  • Add weight only when all sets reach the top of the rep range with good form (+2.5 kg next time).

▼ slow & steady wins
  • Weigh yourself 2× a week, in the morning. Compare weekly averages, not single days.
  • Losing less than ~0.3 kg/week for 2 weeks? Remove ~200 kcal (usually the dinner carbs).
  • Losing more than ~0.8 kg/week? Add ~200 kcal back. Fast loss costs muscle.
  • Banana or coffee ~45 min before the gym works great as fuel.
  • Alcohol and sugary drinks are the easiest calories to cut — they don't fill you up at all.

💡 Real-life tips

  • Meal prep: Sunday — cook 1 kg chicken + big pot of rice. Lunches for 4 days, done.
  • Eating out: pick grilled protein + veg/salad. Kebab plate beats the burger meal. Skip the soft drink.
  • Woolies shortcuts: hot roast chicken (remove skin), microwave rice pouches, frozen veg steam bags, pre-made salad bags.
  • Hungry at night? Greek yogurt or a boiled egg — protein fills you, chips don't.
  • One bad meal changes nothing. A bad week does. Just go back to the plan at the next meal.

📱 Put it on your phone

  • iPhone: open your hosted link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It gets its own icon and opens full-screen like a real app.
  • Android: open the link in Chrome → menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
  • After the first open it works offline too — only the videos need internet.
  • The old way still works: copy this file to any device and open it in Chrome.
  • Each device keeps its own saved data — move it with Export → Import.
  • Deleting the home-screen icon deletes that device's saved data — export a backup first.
