🍑 Glute Builder

How this plan works

  • Train 3 days a week (for example Mon / Wed / Fri) — never two days back-to-back. Glutes grow on the rest days.
  • Day A and Day C are glute days (hip thrusts and hinges). Day B keeps the upper body strong and balanced, and ends with an easy glute pump.
  • Weights will not make you bulky — muscle is exactly what makes glutes round and lifted. Scale down + weights up = smaller waist, fuller glutes.
  • Squeeze the glutes hard at the top of every rep. If you can't feel the glute working, go lighter and slower — feeling it beats lifting more.
  • First session: use the suggested weight shown under each exercise. Beating the top reps easily → go heavier next set. Can't reach the bottom → go lighter.
  • The growth rule: when all sets reach the top of the rep range with good form → +2.5 kg next time. Months of small adds = the result you want.

▼ slow & steady wins
  • Weigh yourself 2× a week, in the morning. Compare weekly averages, not single days.
  • Losing less than ~0.2 kg/week for 2 weeks? Remove ~150 kcal (usually the dinner carbs).
  • Losing more than ~0.7 kg/week? Add ~200 kcal back. Fast loss eats the muscle you're trying to build.
  • On gym days, keep the carbs — especially in the meal before training. Glutes grow best with fuel.
  • When you reach your goal weight, add ~250 kcal back (mostly carbs). That's when glutes grow fastest.
  • Alcohol and sugary drinks are the easiest calories to cut — they don't fill you up at all.

💡 Real-life tips

  • Cook once for two: most dinners here match the other tracker's plan — same food, smaller plate for you, bigger for him.
  • Meal prep: Sunday — cook 700 g chicken + a pot of rice. Lunches for 4 days, done.
  • Eating out: grilled protein + veg/salad. Kebab plate beats the burger meal. Skip the soft drink.
  • Woolies shortcuts: hot roast chicken (remove skin), microwave rice pouches, frozen veg steam bags, salad bags.
  • Hungry at night? Greek yogurt or a boiled egg — protein fills you, chips don't.
  • One bad meal changes nothing. A bad week does. Just go back to the plan at the next meal.

A small health note

Lifting at 46 is one of the best things you can do — it protects bone density and keeps the metabolism up while you lose weight. Sore muscles for a day or two is normal and good. Sharp pain in a knee or the lower back is not: drop the weight, shorten the range, and if it lasts more than a few days, check with a GP.
